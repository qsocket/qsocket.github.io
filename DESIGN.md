# Forest Fidelity Design System

## Brand & Style
Forest Fidelity adopts a grounded, high-fidelity design style built for clarity and focus in a dark environment. The visual language emphasizes deep tones, reliability, and precision.

## Colors
- **Color Mode**: Dark
- **Primary**: `#57815b` (Sage green for primary actions)
- **Secondary**: `#6a7c6a` (Earthy grey-green)
- **Tertiary**: `#411627` (Deep dark burgundy accent)
- **Neutral**: `#767873` (Balanced stone neutral)

## Typography
- **Family**: Inter (Headline, Body, and Label)

## Layout & Spacing
- **Gutters**: 1rem
- **Margins**: 1.5rem
- Standard spacing scale applied consistently across components.

## Elevation & Depth
Utilizes tonal surface layers and low-opacity ambient shadows optimized for dark mode interfaces.

## Shapes
- **Roundedness**: Level 2 (Rounded, 0.5rem base radius)

## Components
Buttons, inputs, cards, and navigation elements styled consistently with the rounded geometry and dark forest theme.
